Introduction

Nujoud General Contracting (“we”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or submit an enquiry through our website or social media lead forms.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as:

  • Your name, telephone number, and email address
  • Company and job details
  • Information about your construction or contracting requirements
  • Any other details you voluntarily provide through enquiry forms, Facebook or Instagram Lead Ads, WhatsApp, phone, email, or other communication channels

Where applicable, we may also collect technical information about your website visit, such as your browser, device, and usage data.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Prepare quotations and discuss project requirements
  • Provide information about our services
  • Arrange consultations and follow-up communications
  • Manage customer relationships
  • Improve our website and services

We may also use your information for marketing communications where permitted by applicable law and any required consent has been obtained.

Facebook and Instagram Lead Forms

If you submit your details through a Facebook or Instagram Lead Form, the information you provide may be shared with Nujoud General Contracting so that we can respond to your enquiry and discuss relevant services. We handle this information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements.

Your use of Facebook or Instagram is also subject to the respective platform’s terms and privacy policies.

Sharing of Information

We may share personal information with authorized employees and service providers where reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, operate our business, maintain our website, or provide our services. We may also disclose information where required by law or by a competent authority.

We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where applicable. To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact details below.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics (Google tag) to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed. This may involve cookies or similar technologies that collect technical and usage information. Our website also loads fonts and icon libraries from third-party services (such as Google Fonts and Font Awesome), which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. You can also use Google’s opt-out tools to limit Google Analytics tracking.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with the revised “Last Updated” date.
